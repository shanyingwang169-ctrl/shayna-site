PRODUCTION FLOW

Six stages, one roof.

01

Incoming QC

LED bins, drivers and aluminum castings sampled and logged per batch.

02

SMT

Two automated pick-and-place lines, reflow + AOI inspection on every panel.

03

Assembly

Four stations, torque-controlled, thermal paste applied by jig.

04

Aging test

48-hour burn-in racks, 100% of production, logged by serial number.

05

Photometric test

Integrating sphere every lot; goniophotometer for new models.

06

Packing

Export cartons drop-tested to ISTA; palletized, container-direct.
