QUALITY & CERTIFICATES

Built to spec. Tested to fail.

Every luminaire passes five test stations before it ships. Certificates are documented, current and available with your quote.

TEST STATIONS

Five gates before packing.

  • Gate 1 — Incoming: LED bin test, driver sample burn-in
  • Gate 2 — SMT: AOI + first-article check per shift
  • Gate 3 — Photometric: integrating sphere every lot, flux & CCT recorded
  • Gate 4 — Safety: hipot 2.5 kV, ground continuity, leakage < 0.5 mA
  • Gate 5 — Aging: 48 h burn-in, 100% of units, serial-logged
