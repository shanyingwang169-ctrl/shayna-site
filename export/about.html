ABOUT HARBORLUX

A real factory, run by engineers.

HARBORLUX is the export brand of Ningbo Harbor Lighting Co., Ltd. — making industrial LED fixtures in Beilun since 2013. We design, assemble and test everything under one roof.

OUR STORY

From one assembly bench to 500k units a year.

We started in 2013 with a single assembly bench making high bay fixtures for a Zhejiang logistics group. Today we run two automated SMT lines and four assembly stations, exporting to more than 40 countries. We stayed deliberately focused: industrial and outdoor LED only — no consumer products, no trading-company games. When you email us, an engineer answers.
