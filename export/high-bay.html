FAQ

High bay buying questions

What is the MOQ and can I mix wattages?

MOQ is 50 pcs per order. Wattages and CCTs can be mixed within one order at no extra cost above 100 pcs total.

Do you provide IES files and DIALux layout?

Yes — IES/LDT files for every wattage are included with the quote, and we run a free DIALux simulation for your floor plan before you commit.

Can I get a sample before the bulk order?

Yes. Samples ship in 5–7 days; sample cost is deducted from the bulk order above 200 pcs.

What driver brands do you offer?

Standard is Sosen; Meanwell HLG is optional (approx. +12% unit cost). Both carry their own manufacturer warranty on top of ours.
