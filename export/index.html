WHY HARBORLUX

Engineering, capacity and traceability under one roof.

01 · ENGINEERING

In-house photometric design

Every fixture is designed in DIALux and validated in our photometric lab. IES/LDT files ship with every quote, so your specifiers can check uniformity and lux before ordering.

02 · MANUFACTURING

Own SMT lines, no outsourcing

Driver boards are placed, reflowed and AOI-inspected on our own lines. Batch traceability from LED reel to finished carton — the audit trail procurement teams ask for.

03 · DELIVERY

Container-direct, 25–30 days

500k units annual capacity across four assembly stations. FCL pricing, export cartons drop-tested to ISTA standards, and consolidated shipping from Ningbo port, 25 minutes away.

Inside the plant
