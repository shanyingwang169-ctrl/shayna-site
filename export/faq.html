FAQ

Questions buyers actually ask.

Straight answers on MOQ, lead time, samples, payment and warranty. Anything missing — email us, an engineer replies.

ORDERING

What is your MOQ?

50 pcs per order for high bay, flood and street series; 100 pcs for panels. Mixed wattages and CCTs are fine within one order above 100 pcs total.

Can I order samples first?

Yes. Samples ship in 5–7 days by DHL/FedEx. Sample cost is deducted from your bulk order above 200 pcs.

What are the payment terms?

T/T: 30% deposit, 70% against B/L copy. LC at sight for orders above $50,000. Trade terms: FOB Ningbo, CIF on request.

What is the lead time?

25–30 days after deposit for standard models. OEM orders add 5–7 days; full ODM housings take 45–60 days including tooling.

PRODUCT & QUALITY

Which driver brands do you use?

Standard is Sosen; Meanwell HLG optional (+12% approx.). Both are isolated, PF > 0.95, with 4 kV surge protection, and carry their own factory warranty on top of ours.

Do you provide IES files and DIALux layouts?

IES/LDT files for every wattage come with the quote. Send your floor plan and we run a free DIALux simulation showing lux levels and fixture positions before you order.

What warranty do you offer?

5 years on all series — LED, driver and housing — under normal use. Failed units are replaced in your next shipment or spares shipped free, decided within 48 hours of claim. Trailing 24-month defect rate: under 0.3%.

Can you match my market's certification requirements?

CE, RoHS, SAA and IEC 62471 are current; ENEC is in progress (ETA 2026). For other markets (SASO, INMETRO, BIS), we work with local labs — tell us the market and we'll confirm feasibility and cost.

SHIPPING & AFTERSALES

How do you pack for sea freight?

Export cartons drop-tested to ISTA standard, 4 pcs per carton for high bays. Palletized on request; container loading photos and reports sent before the truck leaves.

What if a unit fails in the field?

Email a photo + serial number. We respond within 48 hours: replacement in your next order, or free spare drivers/LED boards shipped with your next shipment — your choice.

Do you offer private label / OEM?

Yes — logo printing from 200 pcs, custom cartons from 500 pcs, full ODM housings from 3,000 pcs. See the OEM/ODM page for the full option and MOQ table.

Tell us about your project.

Send specs — fixture type, quantity, target market. A real engineer replies within one business day with a quote, IES files and lead time.
