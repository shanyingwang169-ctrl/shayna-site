AURELLE / About

Started with one weaving frame.

In 2012 our founder wove the first Riviera prototype by hand for a German buyer who wanted hospitality quality at retail prices.

That order became a workshop. The workshop became 15,000 square metres across four linked departments. The habit stuck: we still tension-check every seat by hand, and we still answer every email ourselves.

Two hundred and fifty thousand pieces a year now leave for sixty countries, most of them to customers who started with a single sample set. We have never sold a container to someone who had not first touched a weave.
