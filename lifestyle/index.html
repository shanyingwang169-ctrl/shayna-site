How a set gets made.

  1. FrameCut, TIG-welded on a jig, powder-coated in-house in any RAL colour.
  2. WeaveHand-woven flat PE rattan, tension checked every two hours against the sample.
  3. UpholsteryCushions cut from 28 kg/m³ foam, covered in olefin or acrylic, logged by batch.
  4. InspectionAQL 2.5 final inspection with a photo report before a carton is sealed.
  5. LoadingKnock-down packing, container loading supervised and photographed.

Questions buyers actually ask.

What is the minimum order?

Five sets, and you can mix sofa, dining and lounger families in one container. Umbrellas start at twenty pieces, bistro sets at twenty.

Can we order samples first?

Yes. Samples ship in seven to twelve days and the cost is credited against orders above twenty sets. Swatch cards are free.

How long will it last outdoors?

Five-year frame warranty, three years on weave and stitching. Our desert and marine specifications are running three seasons with under half a per cent claims.

What if something fails in the field?

Send a photograph and the batch number. Within forty-eight hours you get spares in the next container, a replacement, or a credit.

Do you do private label?

Woven logos from fifty sets, retail packaging from two hundred, full design development from three hundred.

Tell us what you are furnishing.

Send the market, the look and the volume. A designer answers within one business day with a shortlist and a rendering.
