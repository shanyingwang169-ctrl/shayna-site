Four workshops, one attention span.

Aluminium, powder coat, weave and upholstery sit in four linked buildings in Lecong. Nothing critical leaves the site.

The frame comes first

Tubes are cut on a CNC saw, welded in jigs and checked for square before they reach a weaver. A frame that is two millimetres out will fight the weave for the rest of its life.

1.5 mm aluminiumRobotic weldingSquare-checked per jig

Then the hands

Eighty weavers, five to ten years each at the frame. Tension is checked by touch every two hours, and again by hand on every seat before packing.

Started with one weaving frame.

In 2012 our founder wove the first Riviera prototype by hand for a German buyer who wanted hospitality quality at retail prices.

That order became a workshop. The workshop became 15,000 square metres across four linked departments. The habit stuck: we still tension-check every seat by hand, and we still answer every email ourselves.

Two hundred and fifty thousand pieces a year now leave for sixty countries, most of them to customers who started with a single sample set. We have never sold a container to someone who had not first touched a weave.

Questions buyers actually ask.

Minimums, materials, lead times and what happens when something fails in year three.

Ordering

What is the minimum order?

Five sets, and you can mix sofa, dining and lounger families in one container. Umbrellas start at twenty pieces, bistro sets at twenty.

Can we order samples first?

Yes. Samples ship in seven to twelve days and the cost is credited against orders above twenty sets. Swatch cards are free.

What are the payment terms?

Thirty per cent deposit, seventy against the bill of lading. Letter of credit at sight above fifty thousand dollars.

How long is production?

Thirty to forty-five days from deposit. Custom colours add five to seven days; a new design takes forty-five to sixty including tooling.

Materials and quality

What is the furniture made of?

Powder-coated aluminium frames, UV-stabilised flat PE rattan, olefin or acrylic cushions, tempered glass tops, FSC teak when specified.

How long will it last outdoors?

Five-year frame warranty, three years on weave and stitching. Our desert and marine specifications are running three seasons with under half a per cent claims.

Are you audited for retail chains?

BSCI audited with an A rating in 2025, plus ISO 9001. Reports are shared with every quote.

Can you meet our market standards?

EN-581 is standard. For AS/NZS, ASTM or other marks we test through TÜV or SGS — tell us the destination and we confirm cost before you order.

Shipping and after-sales

How is it packed?

Knock-down flat cartons, forty-eight sofa sets per forty-foot container. Retail barcoded cartons optional. Loading photographs before every truck leaves.

What if something fails in the field?

Send a photograph and the batch number. Within forty-eight hours you get spares in the next container, a replacement, or a credit. Two per cent free spares ship with every order.

Do you do private label?

Woven logos from fifty sets, retail packaging from two hundred, full design development from three hundred.
