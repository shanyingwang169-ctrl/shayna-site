Shade that survives the wind

A vented crown, a pole that does not flex, and canvas dyed through rather than printed on.

What you are buying

Printed canvas fades from the outside in. Solution-dyed acrylic is the same colour through the fibre, so a third summer looks like the first.

The crown is vented: wind passes through instead of lifting the whole umbrella off its base, which is how most terrace umbrellas actually die.

Bases ship separately by sea — cheaper freight, and you choose the weight your site needs.
